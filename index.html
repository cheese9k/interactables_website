The assistant

Describe the session. It drafts it. You apply.

One sentence becomes slides, live questions, timings that add up and speaker notes. Then edit by chat: "shorter and plainer", "add timings for 90 minutes".

  • It proposes, and nothing changes until a person applies it
  • It can only edit the template you have open: no web access, nothing else
  • Every turn is logged: who, when and which tools

Meetings and calendar

Link it to the Teams meeting and it helps fill itself in.

Most sessions run inside a meeting, or have one booked around them. Interactables works with Microsoft Teams before, during and after.

  1. Before

    Start from the invite

    Your next fortnight of client meetings appears on Home. Build one with the assistant and it starts from the client, the date, the attendees and the agenda.

  2. During

    Suggestions for the slide on screen

    Type what you hear, or follow the meeting chat. The assistant suggests rows, notes, actions and open questions.

  3. After

    The recap finds its place

    When the Teams recap or transcript is ready, the assistant proposes where each note and action belongs, with its source.

    Built for other people's boardrooms.

    Participants never need an account

    Room screens open from a view-only link. Phones vote with a code. Speaker notes and meeting notes never go to either.

    Anonymous votes

    No name, email or IP address is stored with a vote. A phone can change its vote but can't vote twice, and votes expire after 30 days.

    A person applies everything

    The assistant and the meeting suggestions propose. Nothing is saved, and nothing reaches the screen, until someone accepts it.

    Workspaces are kept apart

    Your clients, templates and sessions belong to your workspace. Isolation is enforced on every request and covered by tests.

    Your client's data is theirs

    Export everything held for a client, or delete it. An activity log records who did what.

    Connects to your tools

    Microsoft 365 for calendar, meetings and usage evidence. Webhooks for everything else. An MCP server so your AI assistant can answer "what happened in my last workshop?"
